01 — THE QUESTION

WHAT HAPPENS
TO THE
CITIZEN?

Bangladesh is a delta. Rivers divide land, create land, destroy land and create new life. Political power can behave in the same way. It can build institutions. It can also erode them. The citizen stands where those forces meet.

02 — THE POSITION

NOT ANTI-REFORM. ANTI-CAPTURE.

We oppose constitutional abuse. We oppose constitutional capture. We believe constitutional reform can be necessary, but reform must strengthen citizenship rather than become a mechanism for temporary political control.

03 — THE POWER MAP

WHO SERVES
WHOM?

Imagine Bangladesh as a river system. Political power flows through government, institutions and law. But the constitutional system should ultimately flow toward the citizen.
