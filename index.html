YOUR LIFE IS CINEMA.

Personal Cinema transforms lived experience, digital memory, imagination and artificial intelligence into cinematic expression. No studio. No permission. No fixed format. Begin with your life.

01 / THE MOVEMENT

Cinema no longer has to begin with a screenplay. It can begin with a life.

A phone recording. A voice message. A photograph. A journey. A breakup. A parent aging. A political event. A disappearing landscape. A lonely night. These fragments already contain cinema. The filmmaker's task is to discover the film hidden inside life.

05 / AI + IMAGINATION

SHOW WHAT MEMORY CANNOT PHOTOGRAPH.

Artificial intelligence becomes an imagination layer. It can reconstruct a lost room, visualize a childhood, create impossible landscapes, transform dreams, animate photographs or build cinematic spaces that never physically existed.

But the boundary matters: imagination should not be falsely presented as documentary evidence.

FREEDOM
NEEDS RESPONSIBILITY.

Consent

Your experience belongs to you. Other people's identities and private lives require consideration and consent.

Truth

Fiction, reconstruction and AI should not be presented as documentary evidence.

Context

Personal cinema should reveal complexity rather than manipulate vulnerable people.
